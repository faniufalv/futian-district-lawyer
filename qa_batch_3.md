## Q1: [New Company Law] For Overseas Chinese: What legal risks should overseas Chinese be aware of when investing in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese investing in China should be aware of: 1) Foreign Investment Negative List restrictions; 2) Foreign exchange controls requiring compliance approval for profit repatriation; 3) Tax compliance considering China tax law and bilateral tax treaties. We recommend engaging professional lawyers for comprehensive legal due diligence before investment.

**法律依据**: 《外商投资准入负面清单》

**领域**: 涉外投资 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Intellectual Property] For Overseas Chinese: What are the new regulations on registered capital under the new Company Law?

**A**: The 2024 revised Company Law: 1) LLC shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within 5 years from establishment; 2) Joint-stock company promoters must pay upon establishment; 3) State Council may set special provisions. Companies should evaluate capital payment plans promptly.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国公司法》(2024修订)

**领域**: 新公司法 | **语言**: English
